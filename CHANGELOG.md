# Changelog

## Unreleased

- CMS collections and entries: `Collection`, `Entry`, `EntryTranslation` (one per language) and `Revision` models with their tables; `SaveCollection`, `DeleteCollection`, `CreateEntry`, `SaveDraft` (with a version check against overwriting), `PublishEntry` (now, keeping the date, or scheduled), `UnpublishEntry`, `TrashEntry`, `RestoreEntry` and `RestoreRevision` actions; slugs unique per collection and language; the `live()` scope; `config/sunrice-cms.php` with the content languages (`Locales`) and how many revisions to keep. `DeleteBlueprint` refuses a blueprint a collection uses. See `docs/cms/collections.md`.
- CMS fields: blueprints and fieldsets (`sunrice_blueprints`, `sunrice_fieldsets`, `SaveBlueprint`, `DeleteBlueprint`, `SaveFieldset`, `DeleteFieldset`), the field types `text`, `textarea`, `rich_text` (sanitised), `number`, `toggle`, `select`, `date`, `link` (safe URLs only), `media`, `group`, `repeater`, `flexible` and `fieldset` includes, `Schema` (validate, normalise, hydrate, references), `DefinitionValidator`, and your own types with `CmsPlugin::make()->fieldTypes()` (`FieldType`, `CustomField`). The CMS requires `symfony/html-sanitizer`. See `docs/cms/fields.md`.
- AI tools for every resource and settings section: `list_records`, `get_record`, `save_record` (partial updates), `delete_record`, `run_action`, `get_settings`, `update_settings`, plus `list_media` and `get_activity`, with the screens' permissions and validation (now in the `SaveRecord` and `UpdateSettings` actions).
- Fix: read-only AI tokens could still use permissions granted through roles (spatie/laravel-permission's gate check answered first); the read-only check now always runs first.
- AI access: tokens (read-only, expiring) on the AI access page, the MCP endpoint (`/mcp`, laravel/mcp) where AI agents work as the token's user with that user's permissions, `get_panel_info`, and `$panel->tools()` / `aiInstructions()` for plugins' tools (`Sunrice\Admin\Ai\Tool`). Read-only tokens may only view. Changes are logged as the AI agent's; token events are security events and alerts; Users → Revoke AI tokens. See `docs/admin/ai.md`.
- Security alerts by email: an account locked, password spraying, a powerful account signing in from a new address or after a long absence, someone gaining power over users or roles, a dangerous upload, bursts of exports or deletes. Sent to super admins and configured roles and addresses, once per cooldown (`security.alerts`). The `ActivityRecorded` event. See `docs/admin/security.md#email-alerts`.
- Responding to an account in the wrong hands: Users → Sign out everywhere, Lock account and Unlock account (also `sunrice:unlock`), a Locked column, and `AccountGuard` in code. Locked accounts can't log in, and their open sessions end on the next page. Resources gain `Action::visible()` and `Column::state()`.
- Security events in the activity log (source "security"): logins in, out, failed, refused and blocked, accounts locked, password resets, blocked uploads, exports, and changes of roles and permissions. `SecurityLog` for your own, the `SecurityEventRecorded` event, and `security.log_channel` to copy them to a log channel. See `docs/admin/security.md`.
- The activity log: who changed what (resources, users and roles, settings, media), with the fields that changed but never their values, the source (admin, AI agent, system, security) and IP. `ActivityLog::record()` / `updated()` / `withSource()` / `withoutLogging()` for plugins, the Activity log page (`sunrice.activity.view`) with filters and item histories, and `sunrice:activity-prune` (daily, `activity.keep_days`). New `TrashMedia` action. See `docs/admin/activity.md`.
- The `MediaPicker` form field: choose media from the library (folders, search, upload) in resource and settings forms; one id or an ordered list, limited to types. `Media::whereTypes()`. The picker's JSON endpoint `GET /admin/media/picker`, and JSON answers to uploads that ask for them.
- The Media page: folders, upload by drop or picker with progress, search, type filter, details (title, alt text, caption), replace, move, trash and restore. Permissions `sunrice.media.view`, `.upload`, `.update` and `.delete`; `$panel->mediaLibrary(false)` turns it off. `UpdateMedia`, `MoveMedia`, `SaveMediaFolder` and `DeleteMediaFolder` actions. The workbench links its storage and serves at 127.0.0.1:8000.
- Media library storage: `Media` and `MediaFolder` models, `UploadMedia`, `ReplaceMedia` and `DeleteMedia` actions, image sizes (Intervention Image, on the queue if you like) and `sunrice:media-sizes`. Uploads are checked for allowed types, scripts and web pages (by content too) and unsafe SVGs. Config under `sunrice-admin.media`. See `docs/admin/media.md`.
- Settings: sections of typed settings (`Settings` classes with typed properties and defaults, `#[Encrypted]` for secrets), read with one cached query, edited on the panel's Settings page with a permission per section (`$panel->settings()`), and the `SettingsSaved` event. A `Secret` form field. `Field::validationRules()` takes a null record outside resources. See `docs/admin/settings.md`.
- Users and Roles screens (resources under Administration, permissions `sunrice.users.*` and `sunrice.roles.*`), with `PermissionRank`: nobody can manage users or roles with permissions they don't have, delete themselves or change their own roles. Swap or remove them with `$panel->userResource()` and `$panel->roleResource()`. See `docs/admin/users.md`.
- Resources: `Resource::can()` (refuse more than the policy or permissions), `Resource::newRecord()`, `Password` and `CheckboxList` fields, `->disabled()`, `->loadUsing()` and `->saveUsing()` on fields, to-many relationship columns, and filter options from a closure. The panel's own permissions are listed before the app's and the plugins'.
- Resources: admin screens for Eloquent models described in PHP (forms with sections and six field types, lists with search, filters, sorting, row and bulk actions and CSV export), authorized by the model's policy or the resource's permissions. See `docs/admin/resources.md`.
- Roles and permissions with spatie/laravel-permission: its tables ship with the panel, permissions sync from code (`sunrice:sync-permissions`, also after every migrate), a super admin role that may do everything, and `sunrice:make-admin` for the first admin. See `docs/admin/permissions.md`.
- Signing in: login (with remember me), logout and password reset pages; only accounts with `sunrice.access` get in; three-way brute-force throttling (`config/sunrice-admin.php`) and the `LoginThrottled` event. See `docs/admin/auth.md`.
- The admin shell: Inertia + React 19 layout with sidebar navigation, the dashboard, panel routes behind sign-in and `sunrice.access`, `php artisan sunrice:assets`. Plugin pages in React with `@sunrice/admin-sdk` (shared React/Inertia/UI, bundles loaded per page, runtime version check). See `docs/admin/frontend.md`.
- The Panel and plugin contract: `PanelProvider`, `Panel` (path, guard, brand, middleware), plugins with dependency checks, navigation, permissions and shared page data. See `docs/admin/panel.md`.
- Monorepo skeleton: `sunrice/admin` and `sunrice/cms` packages, tests, architecture boundary tests, CI.
