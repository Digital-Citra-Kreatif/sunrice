# Getting started

The panel is where you look after the site and the people who work on it.
What you see depends on your role: the sidebar only lists what you may use.

## Finding your way

- **Dashboard**: what changed lately and what's waiting for you.
- **The sidebar**: everything you may open, in groups. Press Ctrl + B (or ⌘ + B) to fold it away.
- **Your name**, at the bottom of the sidebar: light or dark appearance, and logging out.

## Saving your work

Forms say when something isn't saved yet; leaving the page asks first. If
someone else is editing the same thing, the panel tells you who, so you
don't overwrite each other's work.

## Signing in safely

- Use a password nobody else knows, and that you don't use elsewhere.
- If the site asks for a code after your password, it was emailed to you: it works once, for a few minutes.
- After many wrong passwords the account locks for a while. Ask whoever runs the panel if you need it sooner.
