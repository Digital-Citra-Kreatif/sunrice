# Appearance and branding

**Settings → Branding** gives the panel your organisation's look: its name, logo, typeface and colour, and the wording of the sign-in page. It changes the panel only, never the website. Editing it needs a site manager's role.

Your own **Light**, **Dark** or **System** appearance is chosen in the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar, and is yours alone.

## The panel

| Setting | Where it shows | Left empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Panel name** | The sidebar, the sign-in page, browser tabs and this guide | The name your developer gave the panel |
| **Tagline** | Under the name in the sidebar, and above the sign-in form | Nothing |
| **Logo** | The sidebar and the sign-in page, as **Sidebar shows** says; pick an image from the media library: a square mark, or a wide one (a wordmark) | The name's first letter on the brand colour |
| **Browser icon** | The small picture in browser tabs and bookmarks, and the top of the collapsed sidebar; a square ICO, PNG or SVG from the media library | A square logo, else the name's first letter on the brand colour |
| **Sidebar shows** | The top of the sidebar and the sign-in page: see below | **Automatic** |
| **Font** | Every page of the panel; choose one of the listed typefaces, or **System** for your computer's own. The listed typefaces come from Bunny Fonts on the internet: on a private network without internet access, or if you'd rather browsers didn't contact another company, choose **System** | The panel's usual typeface (Instrument Sans, also from Bunny Fonts) |
| **Brand colour** | Main buttons, the current menu item, focus outlines | Neutral grey |

**Sidebar shows** has a small picture of the sidebar for each choice, drawn with what's in the form before you save:

- **Automatic**: a wide logo instead of the name, a square one beside it. This is how the panel looked before the choice existed.
- **Logo and name**: the logo beside the name and tagline. A wide logo doesn't fit beside the name, so the browser icon (or the first letter) takes its place.
- **Logo only**: the logo alone, at its own shape. Choose it for a logo that already spells out the name. Without a logo the name shows, with the first letter beside it.
- **Icon and name**: the browser icon beside the name and tagline, for a wide logo when you still want the name written out.
- **Name only**: the name and tagline, without a picture.

When the sidebar is collapsed to a row of icons, its top always shows the same square as the browser tab: the browser icon, else a square logo, else the name's first letter.

For the brand colour, type a colour code like `#2563eb` or use the colour picker. The panel puts black or white text on it, whichever is easier to read, and a lighter shade in dark mode. The sample next to the field shows how buttons will look.

## The sign-in page

| Setting | |
| --- | --- |
| **Layout** | A side panel next to the form, or the form alone in the middle. |
| **Small heading**, **Headline**, **Text**, **Note at the bottom** | The words in the side panel. Each new line of the headline starts a lighter line. |
| **Image** | A picture for the side panel, instead of the built-in artwork. |

The login code, forgot password and new password screens use the same look.

## Saving

Click **Save**: the whole page reloads so you see the new look straight away. To go back to the defaults, empty the fields and save again.

> [!TIP]
> Choose a logo that reads well small: the sidebar shows it at about the height of a line of text. A square or wide image with a transparent background works best.
