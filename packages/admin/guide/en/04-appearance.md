# Appearance and branding

**Settings → Branding** gives the panel your organisation's look: its name, logo, typeface and colour, and the wording of the sign-in page. It changes the panel only, never the website. Editing it needs a site manager's role.

Your own **Light**, **Dark** or **System** appearance is chosen in the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar, and is yours alone.

## The panel

| Setting | Where it shows | Left empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Panel name** | The sidebar, the sign-in page, browser tabs and this guide | The name your developer gave the panel |
| **Tagline** | Under the name in the sidebar, and above the sign-in form | Nothing |
| **Logo** | The sidebar and the sign-in page; pick an image from the media library. A square logo sits beside the name; a wide one (a wordmark) replaces the name | The name's first letter on the brand colour |
| **Browser icon** | The small picture in browser tabs and bookmarks; a square ICO, PNG or SVG from the media library | A square logo, else the name's first letter on the brand colour |
| **Font** | Every page of the panel; choose one of the listed typefaces, or **System** for your computer's own | The panel's usual typeface (Inter) |
| **Brand colour** | Main buttons, the current menu item, focus outlines | Neutral grey |

For the brand colour, type a colour code like `#2563eb` or use the colour picker. The panel puts black or white text on it, whichever is easier to read, and a lighter shade in dark mode. The sample next to the field shows how buttons will look.

## The sign-in page

| Setting | |
| --- | --- |
| **Layout** | A side panel next to the form, or the form alone in the middle. |
| **Small heading**, **Headline**, **Text**, **Note at the bottom** | The words in the side panel. Each new line of the headline starts a lighter line. |
| **Image** | A picture for the side panel, instead of the built-in artwork. |

The login code, forgot password and new password screens use the same look.

## Saving

Click **Save**: the whole page reloads so you see the new look straight away. To go back to the defaults, empty the fields and save again.

> [!TIP]
> Choose a logo that reads well small: the sidebar shows it at about the height of a line of text. A square or wide image with a transparent background works best.
