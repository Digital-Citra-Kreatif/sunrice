# People and roles

**Users** and **Roles**, under Administration, decide who may do what.

## Roles

A role is a set of permissions, like *editor* or *author*. Give people the
roles they need, nothing more. You can only give out roles whose
permissions you have yourself.

## When someone leaves

Open their account: it shows their last login and what they own. Then:

- **Lock** the account to stop them signing in, keeping everything as it is, or
- **Delete** it: what they owned (their files, the entries they wrote) passes to you.

**Sign out everywhere** ends their sessions on every device; **Revoke AI
tokens** stops their AI agents.

## Security emails

Super admins get an email when something looks wrong: an account locked
after wrong passwords, a sign-in from a new address, many deletions at
once. Each says what happened and what to do. Settings → Security chooses
who else gets them.
